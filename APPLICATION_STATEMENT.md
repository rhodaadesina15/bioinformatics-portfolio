# Interests and Learning Objectives

I am a Biomedical Science graduate preparing to begin an MSc in Bioinformatics at the University of Leicester. My main interests are bioinformatics, multi-omics, artificial intelligence and the use of computational approaches to better understand biological data and disease.

My undergraduate EEG research introduced me to computational analysis of biological data using Python and RStudio and strengthened my interest in combining biology, data science and healthcare.

Through the Bioinformatics & AI Research Internship, I would like to develop practical experience analysing genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic and metabolomic datasets. I want to strengthen my Python and R skills, learn to build reproducible bioinformatics workflows, develop my understanding of machine learning for biological data, and become more confident using Git and GitHub.

I am particularly interested in learning how computational findings can be translated into meaningful healthcare insights in areas such as biomarker discovery, disease understanding, patient stratification and precision medicine. My goal is to use this experience alongside my MSc to build a strong technical foundation and portfolio for a career in bioinformatics and computational healthcare.
